- **Diagnose in Adventure battles:** a lesson with a case file now asks its question when the fight starts, and
  **Diagnose** lists its answer with two wrong ones (leads its session ruled out, or other cases' answers). The right
  pick is the fight's best move; a wrong one costs the turn and says why. Your first pick in each is kept on your own
  computer, never on a hub, and changes no level ([Diagnose](docs/party.md#diagnose)).
