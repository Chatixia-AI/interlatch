- **A group's mindmap and connections:** a group's **Map** tab adds **Mindmap** (the glossary map for just the group's
  projects) and **Connections** (a graph of its projects and terms you walk through by clicking). Both appear once you
  press **Analyze**, which has the analyzer check the links the glossary lists between the group's terms, keep the
  real ones and name each relation ([Project groups](docs/dashboard.md#project-groups)).
