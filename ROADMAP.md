# Roadmap

[← Interlatch](README.md) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md) · 日本語: [ROADMAP.ja.md](ROADMAP.ja.md)

The roadmap now lives in Linear, in the [Interlatch project](https://linear.app/adrian-tam/project/interlatch-7d342b5ae9ba),
grouped as Now, Next and Later. Nothing there has a date, items move as plans change, and finished work moves to the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md). To ask for something or report a problem, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/Chatixia-AI/interlatch/issues); it is mirrored into Linear.

Three rules shape everything on it. Interlatch stays local: no server of ours, no telemetry, no account. Analysis
runs on your own account: your Claude Code or Codex login, or your own API key for IBM Bob or a model provider,
never a key we hold. And Interlatch should say what it doesn't know rather than guess.
